Cyberscope Browser Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Developer: Deondre Henderson · Contact: cyberscope.support@gmail.com

The short version

Cyberscope Browser has no account, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any information about you. What the app saves stays on your headset, and you can delete it at any time.

Information the app stores on your headset

To work, the app keeps some information in its private storage on your Meta Quest headset. None of it is sent to the developer.

  • Browsing: your open tabs, bookmarks, your history of visited pages, the last page you visited, your search engine choice, and per-site settings (such as desktop mode, ad blocker exceptions and the video format you chose for a site).
  • Website data: cookies, logins and cached files that websites store through the app's built-in web view, as in any browser.
  • Playback: where you stopped in videos ("Continue watching"), and your player settings such as volume, picture, equalizer and controller buttons.
  • Your servers: the addresses, user names and passwords of network shares and servers you add (SMB, WebDAV, FTP, SFTP). Passwords are encrypted with a key held in the headset's secure keystore. For SFTP servers, the app also remembers each server's identity key so it can warn you if a different machine poses as your server.

The app does not back this information up to the cloud.

The private window and privacy settings

  • The private window runs in a separate part of the app with its own cookies, cache and site data, which are deleted when you close it (or the next time the app starts, if it was stopped). Nothing from it is added to history, saved tabs or Continue watching. When it hands a video to the VR player, its login for that one video goes with it and is not kept.
  • Third-party cookies (cookies that other sites set inside the page you are on) are blocked by default.
  • Global Privacy Control: by default the app tells websites you do not want your data sold or shared. You can turn this off in the menu.
  • Secure connections: by default the app opens pages over HTTPS when a site supports it, and asks before opening an insecure page.

On-device AI

AI passthrough and 2D to 3D run entirely on your headset, using models that ship inside the app. Video frames are processed in memory and are not saved, uploaded or shared. Nothing is downloaded when you use these features.

Camera, microphone and location

The app does not access the headset's camera images, microphone or location. Passthrough (seeing your room) is drawn by the headset's own system; the app never receives those images. If a website asks for your camera, microphone or location, the app refuses automatically, so websites can't access them through Cyberscope Browser.

If you use hand tracking, the app receives your hand positions from the headset so you can point and pinch. They are used in the moment and never saved or sent anywhere.

Network connections

The app connects only to:

  • the websites you choose to visit, and the video streams they serve;
  • the network shares and servers you add yourself.

The ad and tracker blocker uses a list built into the app; it does not send the addresses you visit anywhere.

When you search from the address bar, your search words go to the search engine you choose (DuckDuckGo unless you pick another), under that search engine's own privacy policy.

Websites you visit have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. Please read the policies of the sites you use.

Meta

The app is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store and runs on Meta's platform. Meta may collect information under its own privacy policy, for example to confirm that you own the app. The developer does not receive personal information from Meta about you, other than overall figures shown in the developer dashboard, such as sales and install counts.

Copy-protected video

The app does not decrypt or work around copy protection (DRM). Protected videos play in the website's own player.

Deleting your information

You can delete what the app stores at any time:

  • clear Continue watching from the start page;
  • remove bookmarks, tabs and saved servers inside the app;
  • use Clear browsing data in the menu to delete history, cookies and site data, and cached files, or switch on clearing them each time the app closes; remove single pages from History;
  • or uninstall the app, which removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is intended for teens and adults (13+) and is not directed at children under 13. Because the developer collects no personal information, none is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever involves collecting information, it will be described clearly before it takes effect.
